Seiya's Journey: The Pegasus Knight

The Beginning of a Legend

Seiya's journey begins as a young orphan in Japan, sent to Greece to train and earn the legendary Pegasus Bronze Cloth. His determination and unwavering spirit help him overcome grueling trials and emerge as a true Saint of Athena.

The Battle for Sanctuary

Upon his return, Seiya and his fellow Bronze Saints discover a conspiracy within the Sanctuary itself. They must fight their way through the Twelve Houses of the Zodiac, guarded by the powerful Gold Saints, to save Athena and restore justice.

Beyond the Sanctuary

The battles do not end there. Seiya and his friends face new threats from the frozen lands of Asgard and the underwater kingdom of Poseidon. With each battle, their cosmos grows stronger, and their bonds of friendship deepen.
