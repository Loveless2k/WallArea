The Knights of the Zodiac

Meet the Legendary Saints

Discover the heroes who burn their cosmos to protect Athena and fight for justice across the world. Each Saint is a warrior of legend, protected by their mystical Cloth, a representation of a celestial constellation.

Dragon Shiryu

Protected by the Dragon constellation, Shiryu is known for his incredible endurance and loyalty. He trained at the Five Old Peaks of Lushan in China and possesses one of the strongest defenses among the Saints, the Dragon Shield.

Cygnus Hyoga

From the frozen lands of Siberia, Hyoga is the Cygnus Saint. He is a master of ice-based attacks, capable of freezing his enemies solid. Despite his cool demeanor, he is a passionate warrior who fights for his friends and his late mother.

Andromeda Shun

Shun is the Andromeda Saint, a pacifist by nature who dislikes fighting. However, he possesses an immense and powerful cosmos. His Andromeda Chain is a versatile weapon, capable of incredible defense and powerful attacks when necessary.

Phoenix Ikki

The lone wolf of the group, Ikki is the Phoenix Saint. He is Shun's older brother and is driven by a fierce, protective instinct. The Phoenix Cloth has the ability to regenerate from ashes, making Ikki a seemingly immortal and formidable foe.
